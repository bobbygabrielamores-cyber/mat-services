A Registered Psychologist (RPsy) is a licensed mental health professional in the Philippines who has passed the Psychologist Licensure Examination administered by the PRC. Under RA 10029, RPsy holders are qualified to conduct psychological assessments, provide counseling and psychotherapy, and deliver other psychological services.

A psychologist holds a degree in psychology and is trained in assessment, counseling, and therapy. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specializes in mental health and can prescribe medication. They often work together to provide comprehensive care — if medication is ever needed, your psychologist can coordinate a proper referral.
