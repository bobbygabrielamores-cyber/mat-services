About the Psychologist

Dedicated to Your

Marl Anthony Tabañag is a licensed Registered Psychologist (RPsy) and Registered Psychometrician (RPm) based in Cebu, Philippines. A proud graduate of the University of the Philippines, he brings rigorous academic training and genuine compassion to every client interaction.

With expertise spanning clinical psychology, psychometrics, and human resource management, Marl bridges the gap between mental wellness and organizational effectiveness. His practice operates under the ethical and professional standards set by Republic Act 10029 — the Philippine Psychology Act of 2009.

Whether you are navigating personal challenges, seeking academic support, or building a healthier workplace culture, Marl provides a safe, non-judgmental space where healing and growth can flourish.

A Registered Psychologist (RPsy) is a licensed mental health professional in the Philippines who has passed the Psychologist Licensure Examination administered by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC). Under RA 10029, RPsy holders are qualified to conduct psychological assessments, provide counseling and psychotherapy, and deliver other psychological services.

A psychologist holds a degree in psychology and is trained in psychological assessment, counseling, and therapy. A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who specializes in mental health and can prescribe medication. Psychologists and psychiatrists often work collaboratively to provide comprehensive mental health care tailored to each client's needs.

A standard counseling or therapy session typically lasts 50 to 60 minutes. Initial intake sessions may be longer (up to 90 minutes) as they involve a comprehensive assessment of your background, concerns, and goals. The number of sessions needed varies depending on the nature and complexity of the concern.
